# Evelyn Marcel | Instructional Design Portfolio

Welcome to my instructional design portfolio.

I design engaging and practical learning experiences that help people develop new skills, understand information, and apply what they have learned in real situations.

My work includes workplace learning, technical training, interactive courses, instructional materials, and 3D learning animations.

## Portfolio Projects

### 1. Giving Constructive Feedback at Work
An interactive workplace learning course designed to help employees communicate feedback clearly, respectfully, and effectively.

**Tools:** Articulate Rise 360

**Learning approach:** Workplace scenarios, interactive activities, and knowledge checks.

### 2. EV High Voltage Diagnostic Communication
An instructor led training guide designed to help learners understand and communicate technical diagnostic information.

**Tools:** PowerPoint

**Learning approach:** Learning objectives, practical scenarios, facilitator guidance, knowledge checks, and reflection activities.

### 3. Hydraulic Cylinder Animation
A 3D learning demonstration illustrating hydraulic cylinder operation, including piston movement, extension, and retraction.

**Tools:** Blender

### 4. Mining Boom Drilling Animation
A 3D animation demonstrating the positioning and operation of a mining drilling boom, including movement, extension, drilling, and withdrawal.

**Tools:** Blender
